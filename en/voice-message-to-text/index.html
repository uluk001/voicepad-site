Telegram voice message to text

A four-minute voice message from a colleague, a video note from a friend, an audio reply in a family chat. No time to listen, no headphones at hand, or you simply read faster than people talk. Forward it to the Voicepad bot in Telegram, or record your own voice right here and get it as text.

Updated 24 October 2026

Turn your voice into text

Tap record, speak, stop: the text appears below with TXT, SRT and DOCX.

Forward it to the bot in Telegram

Three steps in Telegram

  1. Open @Voicepad_bot and tap Start.
  2. Forward the voice message or video note from any chat to the bot, or record a new one right in the chat with the bot.
  3. The text arrives with timestamps. If more than one person spoke, the lines are labelled Speaker 1 and Speaker 2.

No Telegram at hand? Record your voice in the form above or upload an audio file: 3 transcriptions a day work without a login. A recording longer than 3 minutes is not refused: the first 3 minutes are transcribed, and the full text is available after a Telegram login or in the bot.

Buttons under the transcript

Under every transcript in the bot there are buttons: Summary, Tasks, Question (ask anything about the recording), Article, Post, and export to DOCX, TXT and SRT. For a one-minute voice message the text itself is usually enough, but when someone talks through a list of things to do, the Tasks button turns it into a checklist.

Voice messages in a group chat

Add the bot to the chat. Voice messages and video notes up to three minutes long are transcribed automatically for everyone, so nobody has to forward anything. Every chat gets 30 free minutes a month, then minutes come from the balance of whoever sent the recording. If automatic transcription gets in the way, an admin switches it off with /mute and back on with /unmute.

For a busy chat there is chat premium: an admin sends /premium and subscribes for 500 Telegram Stars a month. Replies become bare text, voice messages of any length are transcribed, and the chat gets 300 minutes a month.

Compared with Telegram Premium

Without Premium, Telegram transcribes only a few short voice messages a week. Premium removes that cap but still handles only voice and video messages: no files, no links, no speaker labels, summaries or export. The bot does all of that, and in a group chat it transcribes for every member, with or without Premium.

Languages and quality

The language is detected automatically, no settings needed. Recognition runs on ElevenLabs Scribe v2. The result still depends on the recording: a voice message from a quiet room comes out almost clean, one recorded on a windy street or in a car will have a few guessed words.

Pricing and commands

15 minutes every month are free, which is roughly thirty ordinary voice messages. Packs: 60 minutes for 199 RUB, 300 for 690 RUB, 1000 for 1990 RUB. Minutes never expire, and you pay by card through Tribute with the /buy command. /history lists your recent transcripts and /delete removes them.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, 15 minutes of recordings a month at no cost and with no card. On the site, 3 transcriptions a day work without a login.

Does it work with round video notes?

Yes. Forward a video note the same way as a voice message and you get the same text with timestamps.

Can I transcribe a voice message from a group the bot is not in?

Yes, forward it to the bot in a private chat. If voice messages are a daily thing in that group, adding the bot there is simpler.

Do I need to choose the language?

No. The language is detected automatically, there are no settings.

Where do old transcripts go?

/history lists your recent transcripts. They are kept for 30 days, and /delete removes them sooner when you no longer need them.

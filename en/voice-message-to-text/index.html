Voice message to text online

From WhatsApp, Telegram or a voice recorder. Free, no sign-up.

Transcribe a voice message

Voice message from WhatsApp: how to save it as a file
  1. Tap and hold the voice message. On Android choose Share; on iPhone choose Forward, then the Share icon.
  2. Save it to Files or anywhere else on the phone, then tap Choose a recording in the form above.

Menu names depend on your WhatsApp version. From a computer and in more detail: WhatsApp voice message to text.

How to turn a voice message into text

A four-minute voice message from a colleague or a note on your recorder: there is no time to listen, but the text takes half a minute to read.

  1. Save the voice message as a file. In messengers this is usually Share in the message menu, and you get an .opus, .ogg or .m4a file.
  2. Choose the file in the form above or drag it there. There is nothing to convert.
  3. Get the text by speaker, with timestamps. Copy it or download TXT, DOCX or SRT.

To dictate right on the page, press Record your voice with the microphone. A voice message that came in Telegram is quicker to forward to @Voicepad_bot: it takes voice messages and video notes from any chat.

Voice messages in a group chat

Add the bot to the chat, and voice messages and video notes up to 3 minutes are transcribed automatically for everyone. Each chat gets 30 free minutes a month, then minutes come from the balance of whoever sent the recording. An admin switches this off with /mute and back on with /unmute.

For a busy chat there is chat premium: an admin sends /premium and subscribes the chat for 500 Telegram Stars a month. Replies become bare text, voice messages of any length are transcribed, and the chat gets 300 minutes a month.

Compared with Telegram Premium

Without Premium, Telegram transcribes only a few short voice messages a week. Premium removes that cap but handles only voice and video messages: no files, no links, no speaker labels, summaries or export.

Voicepad needs no Premium, takes voice messages from any messenger and labels speakers. More in Telegram voice messages without Premium.

Questions

Which voice message formats work?

Any format voice messages are saved in: .ogg and .opus from Telegram and WhatsApp, .m4a from iPhone, .mp3 and .wav from a voice recorder. There is nothing to convert.

Can I turn a voice message into text right in Telegram?

Yes, forward the voice message or video note to @Voicepad_bot from any chat. Under the text the bot offers a summary, tasks and DOCX, TXT, SRT files. The bot takes files up to 20 MB; upload a bigger file on the site.

Does it work with round video notes?

Yes. Upload a saved video note on the site or forward it to the bot the same way as a voice message, and you get the same text with timestamps.

Do I need to choose the language?

No. The language of the recording is detected automatically and shown in the result header next to the length.

What happens to voice messages after transcription?

Audio is deleted right after recognition. A result made without logging in stays in server memory for one hour, transcripts in the bot for 30 days, and /delete removes them at once. More in the privacy policy.

How to transcribe Telegram voice messages without Premium

Telegram's built-in voice-to-text gives free accounts a small weekly quota, and the unlimited version comes with a Premium subscription. I build Voicepad, a transcription bot, so keep that bias in mind: below is a sourced look at what Telegram gives you, what Premium solves, and three ways to read voice messages without it.

What Telegram gives free users

Voice-to-text arrived in Telegram as a Premium feature in June 2022 and was extended to round video messages in November 2022. On November 30, 2023, Telegram's blog post "Similar Channels, Reposting Stories, and 9 More Features" opened it to everyone with a cap: users without Premium can convert up to 2 messages per week into text by tapping the →A icon. The same post said the feature would roll out gradually, so it might not appear everywhere at once.

Two details are worth knowing before you build a habit around it:

  • The cap is a server setting. Telegram's API documentation describes free transcription as a trial controlled by two configuration values: a weekly number of transcriptions and a maximum media length in seconds. The sample configuration in the docs shows 300 seconds for that length, but the live value is set by Telegram and I could not confirm it. I found no official announcement after 2023 that changes the figure of two per week, so treat it as likely current, but trust what your app shows over any article, including this one.
  • Groups can unlock it for everyone. Since February 29, 2024, groups can collect boosts, and Telegram's Premium FAQ lists unlimited voice-to-text from group level 6 upward. Telegram's announcement says members of boosted groups get unlimited transcription of voice and video messages sent there, and the API documentation says this does not use their personal weekly quota.

So a free account gets two transcriptions a week in private chats, plus unlimited ones in any group boosted high enough. If a colleague sends you a voice message every working day, the free quota covers two of the five.

What Premium adds and what it still cannot do

Telegram Premium removes the weekly cap. The 2023 announcement says Premium keeps unlimited voice-to-text, and the Premium FAQ lists transcription for both voice messages and video messages. Telegram's February 2024 post adds that Premium includes 4 boosts that can be assigned to any group or channel, which helps a group climb toward level 6. The subscription bundles many features that have nothing to do with voice, so if you would pay for those anyway, unlimited transcription comes as a bonus.

What Premium does not change:

  • It is per account. Your subscription covers you. The people in your chats keep their own limits unless they pay too, or unless the group reaches level 6. Telegram's FAQ says the number of boosts a group needs depends on its total member count, so a large work chat may need several Premium members to boost it.
  • It is built for voice and video messages. That is how Telegram describes the feature everywhere I looked. I found no official wording about audio files sent as documents, YouTube links or recordings that were never in Telegram, so I would not count on it for those.
  • The result is plain text. None of the Telegram pages I read mention timestamps, speaker labels, summaries or export to SRT or DOCX. For a 40-second "running late, start without me" that is all you need. For a 20-minute voice message from a client or a recorded call, you usually want to know who said what and where in the recording it was said.

Option 1: a bot in a private chat

The most direct replacement for the →A button is a transcription bot. Here is how it works with @Voicepad_bot, the bot I build:

  1. Open the bot and tap Start.
  2. Forward the voice message or video note from any chat to it. You can also send an audio or video file, or a link to YouTube, VK Video, RuTube or TikTok.
  3. The text comes back with timestamps and speaker labels. The language is detected automatically.

Every transcript in the bot comes with a summary, a task list, an article, a post and questions about the recording, plus export to DOCX, TXT and SRT. Recordings can be up to 3 hours long, and there are 15 free minutes every month. If you mostly deal with video links rather than voice notes, the guide on YouTube video to text with chapters covers that case.

The honest limits: forwarding is one step more than tapping →A, and minutes are metered rather than unlimited. Files sent to the bot inside Telegram are capped at 20 MB, because Telegram's Bot API documentation sets 20 MB as the maximum file size a bot can download. For bigger files, use the website (Option 3). For a walkthrough with examples, see Telegram voice message to text.

Option 2: a bot in a group that transcribes for everyone

If voice messages pile up in a family chat or a work group, forwarding each one gets old quickly. Add @Voicepad_bot to the chat and it transcribes voice messages and video notes up to 3 minutes long automatically, for everyone in it. Nobody needs Premium, nobody has to forward anything, and people who cannot listen right now (in a meeting, on a train, or hard of hearing) can read along.

Each chat gets 30 free minutes a month; after that, minutes come from the balance of whoever sent the voice message. Admins switch automatic transcription off and on with /mute and /unmute. If the chat needs more, an admin can send /premium and subscribe the chat for 500 Telegram Stars a month: replies become bare text, voice messages of any length are transcribed, and the chat gets 300 minutes a month.

Compare that with boosting the group to level 6. Telegram's February 2024 announcement says groups get boosts from their members or through giveaways, and the FAQ says the number required depends on how many members the group has. If your group already has enough Premium members willing to boost it, Telegram's own transcription becomes free for everyone there and you do not need a bot at all. If it does not, a bot is the practical path. In work chats a long voice message is often a small meeting in disguise; for recorded calls, the guide on meeting recording to notes and action items covers the full workflow.

One thing to do first: tell the group. A bot in a chat processes everyone's voice messages, not only yours. Voicepad deletes the audio right after recognition and keeps transcripts in the bot for 30 days, and members should know that before the bot starts working.

Option 3: the browser (record or upload on the site)

Sometimes the recording is not in Telegram at all, or you are at a laptop with a file. The browser transcription tool on the Voicepad site takes three kinds of input: record your voice, upload a file, or paste a video link.

  • Without login: 3 transcriptions a day, files up to 50 MB, one at a time, no signup. A longer recording is not refused: its first 3 minutes are transcribed, and the full text is available after login or in the bot. Links need a login.
  • Logged in with Telegram (one button): files up to 200 MB, links from YouTube, VK Video, RuTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Dzen, OK and Twitch when the video has a known duration, recordings up to 3 hours (browser recording up to 30 minutes), and the same free minutes and balance as in the bot.

Results can be copied or downloaded as TXT, SRT or DOCX. Logged in, the site is the way around the 20 MB bot limit. Without login it is a quick way to try the quality on a short clip. For a voice message already sitting in a Telegram chat, forwarding it to the bot is faster than saving and uploading it.

Questions

How many voice messages can I transcribe in Telegram for free?

Telegram's announcement of November 30, 2023 says users without Premium can convert up to 2 messages per week into text with the →A icon. The limit is a server setting that Telegram can change, so check what your app shows. In a group boosted to level 6 or above, members can transcribe voice and video messages sent there without using their weekly quota.

Does Telegram Premium transcribe audio files and video links?

Telegram describes Premium voice-to-text for voice messages and video messages. I found no official wording that it covers audio files sent as documents or links to YouTube and other sites. For those, @Voicepad_bot accepts audio and video files and links to YouTube, VK Video, RuTube and TikTok, and the browser tool at voicepad.qupport.io accepts uploads and, after you log in with Telegram, links.

How can everyone in a Telegram group read voice messages without Premium?

There are two ways. The group can reach boost level 6, which unlocks unlimited voice-to-text for its members; boosts come from members with Premium or from giveaways, and the number needed depends on the group size. Or you add @Voicepad_bot to the chat: it transcribes voice messages and video notes up to 3 minutes long for everyone, and each chat gets 30 free minutes a month.

Is there a file size limit when I send a recording to a transcription bot?

Yes. Telegram's Bot API lets a bot download files of up to 20 MB, so files sent to Voicepad inside Telegram are limited to 20 MB. On the Voicepad website you can upload files up to 50 MB without login (a recording longer than 3 minutes is transcribed only for its first 3 minutes), or up to 200 MB after logging in with Telegram.

Is Telegram Premium cheaper than a Voicepad minute pack?

It depends on volume. On October 24, 2026 the App Store listed Telegram Premium at $4.99 in the US and 329 RUB in Russia, and Telegram Premium Annual at $35.99 and 2,390 RUB. A Voicepad pack of 60 minutes costs 199 RUB once, the minutes never expire, and there are also 15 free minutes every month. Premium suits many short voice messages every day; a pack suits uneven use, long recordings, files and exports.

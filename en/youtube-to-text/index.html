YouTube video to text with chapters

A two-hour lecture, an hour-long podcast episode, a conference talk you need one slide from. Watching the whole thing is not an option, but you still need to know what is in it and jump to the right minute. The Voicepad bot in Telegram takes a YouTube link and returns the transcript with timestamps plus a summary that ends with chapters, each one linked to its moment in the video.

How it works

  1. Copy the link to the video. YouTube, VK Video, RuTube and TikTok links all work the same way.
  2. Paste it into the chat with @Voicepad_bot. No sign-up, a Telegram account is all you need.
  3. Wait for the transcript. A short clip takes about a minute, longer videos take proportionally longer.
  4. Tap "Summary". You get the key points and, at the end, a list of chapters with timestamps. Each timestamp opens the video at that moment.

What else you can do with the transcript

Ask a question about the recording ("what did she say about pricing in the second half?") instead of reading the whole text. Pull out action items if it was a work webinar. Turn it into an article or a post for your channel, download SRT subtitles, or save the text as DOCX or TXT. If someone drops a video link into a group chat where the bot is a member, a button appears under the link and every member can open the full transcript in a private chat with the bot.

Accuracy and languages

The language is detected automatically. English and Russian work best, and recordings that switch between the two are fine. Speech recognition runs on ElevenLabs Scribe v2: in our test on a Russian clip full of technical terms it made roughly half as many errors as Whisper-based bots, which turned "backend" into "backing". The quality still depends on the audio: a studio talk comes out clean, a phone recording of a noisy room will have gaps. Names, acronyms and rare terms are worth a quick check before you quote them.

FAQ

Is YouTube to text free?

15 minutes of video every month are free, with no card and no sign-up. Longer videos need a minute pack, the smallest is 60 minutes for 199 RUB.

Do I need to download the video first?

No. Paste the link and the bot does the rest. If the video only opens under your account, download it and send it as a file, up to 20 MB (larger files as a Google Drive or Yandex Disk link).

Can I get subtitles for the video?

Yes, the SRT button under the transcript gives you a subtitle file with timestamps.

Which sites other than YouTube are supported?

VK Video, RuTube and TikTok links work exactly the same way. Any audio or video file up to 20 MB (larger files as a Google Drive or Yandex Disk link) works too.

How long does a one-hour video take?

Processing time grows with the length of the video. A short clip is ready in about a minute, an hour-long one takes proportionally longer.

Does it work with videos in Russian?

Yes, Russian and English are the two languages the bot handles best, and it detects the language on its own.

How to turn a YouTube video into text

An hour-long lecture, a podcast or a long interview: reading is faster than watching, and search finds the right moment.

  1. Copy the link to the video, Shorts included.
  2. Paste it into the field above and log in with Telegram. There is no password: you confirm the login in Telegram itself.
  3. Get the text by speaker, with timestamps. Copy it or download TXT, DOCX or SRT.

You do not need to download the video. It is transcribed in full if it runs up to 3 hours and its length is known. Links from VK Video, RuTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Dzen, OK and Twitch work the same way.

A summary with chapters

The summary comes from the @Voicepad_bot bot. After you log in on the site, the transcript is saved in the bot's history: open it there and tap Summary. For a YouTube video the summary ends with chapters and timecodes, so the right moment in the video is easy to find.

You can skip the site too: send the link to the bot, which takes YouTube, VK Video, RuTube and TikTok links. There you can also turn the recording into tasks, an article or a post, and ask a question about it, such as “what did she say about pricing in the second half?”

Questions

Why do links need a Telegram login?

A video by link is transcribed from your balance of minutes, and that balance is shared with the @Voicepad_bot bot. That is why the transcript is saved in the bot's history, where you can turn it into a summary. There is no password: you confirm the login in Telegram, and 15 minutes a month are free.

How long can the video be?

Up to 3 hours at a time. Only the real length of the video is charged, and nothing is charged if recognition fails.

Does it work with videos in Russian?

Yes. The language is detected automatically, and the text comes in the language of the video.

Can I get subtitles for the video?

Yes, on the site and in the bot you can download an SRT file with a timestamp for every phrase. Upload it to a video editor or to YouTube.

What if the link does not open?

Check that the video is public. If it is private or its length is unknown, download the video and upload it as a file: up to 200 MB on the site after you log in. The bot takes files up to 20 MB; upload a bigger file on the site.

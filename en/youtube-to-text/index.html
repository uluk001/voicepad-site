How it works

  1. Copy the link to the video. YouTube, VK Video, RuTube and TikTok links all work the same way.
  2. Paste it into the form above and log in with Telegram, or send it to @Voicepad_bot. On the site, Vimeo, Dzen, OK and Twitch links work too when the video has a known duration.
  3. Wait for the transcript. It arrives with timestamps, and the processing time grows with the length of the video.
  4. In the bot, tap Summary. You get the key points and, for a YouTube video, a list of chapters with timecodes at the end.

What else you can do with the transcript

Ask a question about the recording (“what did she say about pricing in the second half?”) instead of reading the whole text. Pull out the tasks if it was a work webinar. Turn it into an article or a post for your channel, download SRT subtitles, or save the text as DOCX or TXT. The site gives you the text and the files; the summary, tasks, article, post and questions are buttons under the transcript in the bot.

Recognition and languages

The language is detected automatically, so there is nothing to set. Speech recognition runs on ElevenLabs Scribe v2. The quality still depends on the audio: a studio talk comes out clean, a phone recording of a noisy room will have gaps. Names, acronyms and rare terms are worth a quick check before you quote them.

Long videos and private videos

A video can run up to 3 hours. If it only opens under your account, download it and send it as a file. Inside Telegram the bot accepts files up to 20 MB, which is a Telegram limit; on the site you can upload up to 50 MB without a login and up to 200 MB after a Telegram login.

Pricing

15 minutes every month are free. Minute packs: 60 minutes for 199 RUB, 300 minutes for 690 RUB, 1000 minutes for 1990 RUB. Minutes never expire, you pay for the actual length of the video, and nothing is charged if recognition fails. Pay by card through Tribute with the /buy command in the bot. After a Telegram login, the site uses the same free minutes and balance.

FAQ

Is YouTube to text free?

15 minutes of video every month are free, with no card. Longer videos need a minute pack, the smallest is 60 minutes for 199 RUB.

Do I need to download the video first?

No. Paste the link and the bot or the site does the rest. If the video only opens under your account, download it and upload the file: up to 20 MB in the bot, up to 200 MB on the site after a Telegram login.

Can I get subtitles for the video?

Yes, the SRT button under the transcript gives you a subtitle file with timestamps, in the bot and on the site.

Which sites other than YouTube are supported?

VK Video, RuTube and TikTok links work the same way in the bot and on the site. After a Telegram login, the site also accepts Vimeo, Dzen, OK and Twitch links when the video has a known duration.

Does it work with videos in Russian?

Yes. The language is detected automatically, Russian and English included.

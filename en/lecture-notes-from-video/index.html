How to get notes from a lecture

A ninety-minute lecture recorded on a phone, or a whole course on YouTube, and an exam next week. Rewatching everything is not realistic. What helps is a set of notes to study from and a way to jump straight to the part that did not make sense the first time.

  1. Choose the lecture recording in the form above, or paste a link to YouTube, VK Video or RuTube.
  2. For a full lecture or a link, log in with Telegram: one button, no password. Recordings up to 3 hours are transcribed in full.
  3. Read the text with timestamps, then copy it or download DOCX or TXT.
  4. For the notes, open the transcript in the @Voicepad_bot bot and tap Summary. For a YouTube link, the notes end with chapters and timecodes.

Studying from the transcript

The notes give you the structure; the details live in the full transcript, where every line has a timestamp, so going back to a particular example takes seconds. The bot's Question button answers from the recording: “what did the lecturer say the exam would cover?” or “list the three examples from the second half”.

Recording a lecture yourself

A phone on the front desk gives a clean recording of the lecturer and a clean transcript. A phone in the back row, with chatter around it, gives gaps. Anything on the board or on slides is not in the audio, so the notes cover what was said, not what was shown.

To dictate a summary of your own, press Record your voice with the microphone in the form above. After a Telegram login a recording in the browser can run up to 30 minutes.

Questions

Will a lecture of an hour and a half work?

Yes, recordings up to 3 hours are accepted, as a file or as a link. The bot takes files up to 20 MB; upload a bigger file on the site.

Do I get chapters for a phone recording?

Chapters come with YouTube links. A file transcript has a timestamp on every line, so you can find the moment in your player by hand.

Can I ask questions about the lecture?

Yes, the Question button in the bot answers from the recording.

Does it work for lectures in Russian?

Yes. The language is detected automatically, Russian and English included.

Do the slides end up in the notes?

No, only speech is transcribed. Keep the slides next to the notes for formulas and diagrams.

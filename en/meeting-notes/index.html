How to turn a meeting recording into notes

An hour-long call ends, and everyone leaves with a slightly different idea of who agreed to what. The recording exists, but nobody will watch it again. A transcript by speaker and a short list of action items fix that.

  1. Record the call. Zoom and Google Meet have built-in recording; for a phone call, a voice recorder next to the speaker will do.
  2. Choose the file in the form above or drag it there. A full hour-long call needs a Telegram login: one button, no password, and recordings up to 3 hours are transcribed in full.
  3. Read the text with speaker labels and timestamps, then copy it or download DOCX to send to the participants.

Notes and action items

The notes and the task list come from the @Voicepad_bot bot. After you log in on the site, the transcript is saved in the bot's history: open it there and tap Tasks for the action items, with owners and deadlines where they were said out loud, or Summary for short notes. The Question button answers from the recording, for example “what did we decide about the launch date?”

What affects quality

A Zoom recording with everyone on a headset comes out clean. A phone lying on the table in a meeting room gives more gaps, and when two people talk over each other the boundary between their lines can shift by a word. Check names and numbers before sending the notes around.

Questions

Does it know who is speaking?

Yes, lines are labelled Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on, with a timestamp on each. Replace the labels with names in the DOCX before sending the notes out.

What goes into the action items?

What people promised, took on or asked someone to do, with the owner and the deadline when they were mentioned in the call.

Which recording formats work?

Common audio and video files: MP4, M4A, MP3, WAV and others, so a Zoom or Meet recording goes in as it is. The bot takes files up to 20 MB; upload a bigger file on the site.

How long can a recording be?

Up to 3 hours at a time after you log in with Telegram. Without logging in, the site transcribes the first 3 minutes of a recording, which is enough to check the quality.

Is it safe for work recordings?

Audio is deleted right after recognition. A result made without logging in stays in server memory for one hour, transcripts in the bot for 30 days, and /delete removes them at once. More in the privacy policy.

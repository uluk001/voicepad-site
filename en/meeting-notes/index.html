Meeting recording to notes and action items

An hour-long call ends, and everyone leaves with a slightly different idea of who agreed to what. The recording exists, but nobody will watch it again. The Voicepad bot in Telegram turns that recording into a transcript by speaker, short meeting notes and a list of action items with owners and deadlines where they were said out loud.

Updated 24 October 2026

Send the recording to the bot Upload a file on the site

How to do it

  1. Record the call. Zoom and Google Meet have built-in recording; for a Telegram or phone call, a voice recorder next to the speaker will do.
  2. Send the file to @Voicepad_bot. Inside Telegram a file can be up to 20 MB, which is a Telegram limit. An hour-long call is usually bigger: upload it on the site instead. Without a login the site transcribes only the first 3 minutes, so log in with Telegram (one button): files up to 200 MB, and the transcript is saved to your bot history.
  3. Wait for the transcript. Processing time grows with the length of the recording; recordings up to 3 hours are accepted.
  4. Read the text with timestamps and speaker labels. In the bot, tap Tasks for the action items and Summary for the notes. The DOCX button gives you a document to send to the participants.

If your team talks in a chat

Add the bot to the team chat. Voice messages and video notes up to three minutes are transcribed automatically for everyone. The chat gets 30 free minutes a month, then minutes come from the balance of whoever sent the recording, and an admin can turn automatic transcription off with /mute. With chat premium (/premium, 500 Telegram Stars a month) voice messages of any length are transcribed, the replies come as bare text, and the chat gets 300 minutes a month.

Quality and what affects it

Speakers are labelled Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on, with a timestamp on every line. A Zoom recording with everyone on a headset comes out clean. A phone lying on the table in a meeting room gives more gaps, and when two people talk over each other the boundary between their lines can shift by a word. The language is detected automatically, and recognition runs on ElevenLabs Scribe v2. Check names and numbers before sending the notes around.

Pricing

15 minutes every month are free. A typical hour-long meeting needs a pack: 60 minutes for 199 RUB, 300 minutes for 690 RUB, 1000 minutes for 1990 RUB. Minutes never expire, you pay for the actual length of the recording, and nothing is charged if recognition fails. Pay by card through Tribute with the /buy command in the bot.

FAQ

Does the bot know who is speaking?

Yes, lines are labelled Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on. Replace the labels with names in the DOCX before sending the notes out.

What goes into the action items?

What people promised, took on or asked someone to do, with the owner and the deadline when they were mentioned in the call.

Which recording formats are accepted?

Common audio and video files: MP4, M4A, MP3, WAV and others. In the bot a file can be up to 20 MB; on the site, up to 200 MB after a Telegram login. Without a login the site takes files up to 50 MB but transcribes only the first 3 minutes.

Can I ask a question about the meeting?

Yes, the Question button in the bot answers from the recording, for example “what did we decide about the launch date?”.

Is it safe for work recordings?

Audio is deleted right after recognition. Transcripts in the bot are kept for 30 days, and /delete removes them at once. The details are in the privacy policy.

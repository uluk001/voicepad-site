Questions and answers

  1. Which files can I transcribe, and how long can they be?

    Voice messages, video notes, audio and video in MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, OPUS, MP4, MOV, WEBM and other formats, up to 3 hours long. In the bot a file can be up to 20 MB; on the site, up to 50 MB without a login and up to 200 MB after one. Recording in the browser after a login runs up to 30 minutes.

  2. What if my file is larger than 20 MB?

    That limit comes from Telegram itself: the bot can't receive a file over 20 MB. Upload it on this page instead: up to 50 MB without a login, up to 200 MB after a Telegram login. A video from YouTube, VK Video, RuTube or TikTok can go to the bot as a link.

  3. Can I transcribe a recording without signing up?

    Yes, on this page: 3 transcriptions a day, files up to 50 MB, one at a time. A file with no sound counts as a try, and a failure on our side gives the try back. If a recording is longer than 3 minutes, the first 3 minutes are transcribed, and the full text is available after a Telegram login or in the bot. Logging in takes one button, and in the bot you just tap Start.

  4. How do I transcribe a video from a link?

    Send the link to the bot: it accepts YouTube, VK Video, RuTube and TikTok. On the site links work after a Telegram login, and besides those four, Vimeo, Dzen, OK and Twitch work too when the video has a known duration. The summary of a YouTube video ends with chapters and timecodes.

  5. Do I need to choose the language?

    No, the language is detected automatically. Speech is recognised by ElevenLabs Scribe v2.

  6. How is Voicepad different from Telegram's own transcription?

    Without Premium, Telegram transcribes only a few short voice messages a week. Premium removes that cap but still handles only voice and video messages: no files, no links, no speaker labels, summaries or export. Voicepad also takes audio and video files and video links, adds timestamps and speaker labels, writes a summary and a task list, exports DOCX, TXT and SRT, and works on the site and in group chats.

  7. How do I buy minutes?

    In the bot, with the /buy command. Packs: 60 minutes for 199 RUB, 300 minutes for 690 RUB, 1000 minutes for 1990 RUB, paid by card through Tribute. Minutes never expire, and 15 minutes are free every month.

  8. What happens to my recordings?

    Audio and video are deleted right after recognition. Transcripts in the bot are kept for 30 days, and /delete removes them at once. A result on the site without a login lives only in server memory, for one hour.

  9. How do I add the bot to a group chat?

    Add @Voicepad_bot to the chat: it transcribes voice messages and video notes up to 3 minutes for every member automatically. Each chat gets 30 free minutes a month, after that minutes come from the balance of whoever sent the recording. Admins switch automatic transcription off and on with /mute and /unmute.

  10. What does chat premium include?

    An admin sends /premium in the chat and subscribes for 500 Telegram Stars a month. The bot's replies become bare text, voice messages of any length are transcribed, and the chat gets 300 minutes a month.

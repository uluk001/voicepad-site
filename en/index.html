Questions

Why log in with Telegram?

After you log in, you can upload files up to 200 MB and paste links from YouTube, VK Video, RuTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Dzen, OK and Twitch, and recordings up to 3 hours are transcribed in full. 15 minutes a month are free, after that minutes come from a pack. Your minutes are shared with the @Voicepad_bot bot, and transcripts are saved in its history. There is no password: you confirm the login in Telegram.

How do I get a voice message out of WhatsApp?

On iPhone, tap and hold the voice message, choose Forward, then the Share icon and Save to Files. On Android, tap and hold it, then Share, and save it to Files. Then press Choose a recording in the form at the top of the page. On a computer, choose Download in the message menu and drag the file into the form. The .opus or .m4a format works as it is. You can also forward the voice message to @Voicepad_bot in Telegram: the bot takes files up to 20 MB; upload a bigger file on the site.

Do I need to choose the language?

The language of the recording is detected automatically, so there is nothing to choose. It is shown in the result header next to the length.

What happens to recordings after transcription?

Audio and video are deleted right after recognition. A result made without logging in stays in server memory for one hour, and transcripts in the bot for 30 days. More in the privacy policy.

How do I pay?

Minutes are bought in the bot with the /buy command, by card through Tribute. Packs: 60 minutes for 199 RUB, 300 minutes for 690 RUB, 1000 minutes for 1990 RUB. Minutes never expire. Only the real length of a recording is charged, and nothing is charged if recognition fails.

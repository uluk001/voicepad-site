How to get a podcast transcript

Show notes need timestamps, the website needs a text version so the episode shows up in search, the newsletter needs two good quotes, and the video version needs captions. Doing all of that by hand for a fifty-minute episode takes an afternoon.

  1. Export the episode as MP3, M4A or WAV, or take the video export. If the episode is already on YouTube, copy the link instead.
  2. Choose the file in the form above or paste the link. A full episode and any link need a Telegram login: one button, no password.
  3. Read the transcript: host and guest are labelled Speaker 1 and Speaker 2, and every line has a timecode. Download DOCX or TXT for editing, or SRT for captions.

Timecodes for show notes

If the episode is on YouTube, the summary in the @Voicepad_bot bot ends with chapters: a timecode and a short title, ready to paste into the description. After you log in on the site, the transcript is saved in the bot's history, so open it there and tap Summary. If you uploaded a file, every line of the transcript still has a timecode, so a chapter list is a matter of scrolling through and noting where the topic changes.

Quotes and speaker names

To find a quote, use the bot's Question button: “what did the guest say about hiring?” returns the answer, and the timecode leads to the exact wording in the transcript. Speaker labels are generic, so replace them with names in one find-and-replace pass in the DOCX before publishing. Check guest names, titles and numbers too, especially when a remote guest was on a poor connection.

Questions

Does it tell the host and the guest apart?

Yes, lines are labelled Speaker 1 and Speaker 2 with a timecode on each. Rename the labels in the DOCX before publishing.

Can I get chapters for the show notes?

For an episode on YouTube, yes: the bot's summary ends with chapters and timecodes. For a file, use the timecodes in the transcript.

Which file formats work?

MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, MP4, MOV and other audio and video formats. The bot takes files up to 20 MB; upload a bigger file on the site.

Can I get captions for the video version?

Yes, the SRT button under the transcript gives you a subtitle file with timestamps, on the site and in the bot.

Does it work for episodes in other languages?

Yes. The language is detected automatically, so there is nothing to set, and the text comes in the language of the episode.
